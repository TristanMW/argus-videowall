Connect to your Argus box

Camera setup and streaming are handled entirely by the Argus box — there is nothing to configure here until it's connected. Find it on your network, or enter its address.

Add each camera's RTSP URL below. Save & apply pushes the changes to the box's streaming engine live — then head back to the video wall. Tip: test a URL in VLC first (Media → Open Network Stream).

📋 RTSP URL examples by camera brand

Replace user, pass and the IP with your camera's. Exact paths vary by model/firmware — the camera's web UI or manual has the final word, and VLC is the quickest test.
